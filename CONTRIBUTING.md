# Contributing to the review

Thank you for reading the preprint. This page explains how to comment, how comments are handled and how contributors are credited.

## Ways to comment

- **Issue forms** ([New issue](https://github.com/matkowsky/payment-Fraud/issues/new/choose)): a review comment, answers to the practitioner questions, prior work to cite, or an error report.
- **Line-by-line review** ([review pull request](https://github.com/matkowsky/payment-Fraud/pull/1)): comment directly on a paragraph of the version 1.0 manuscript or supplement. In "Files changed", hover over a line and click the plus sign. Each paragraph is one line.
- **A link to a passage in an issue:** open the manuscript's [Markdown file](paper/v1.0/MANUSCRIPT_v1.0_2026-10-29.md), switch to "Code" view, click a line number, then use "Copy permalink" and paste the link into your issue.
- **Discussions** ([Discussions](https://github.com/matkowsky/payment-Fraud/discussions)): open questions that are not about one passage.
- **Without a GitHub account:** comment on the LinkedIn post or email Jonathan@matkowsky.com.

## What helps most

- Name the version 1.0 section, table or file, and quote the sentence when you can.
- Say whether the point comes from public sources, published research or general practitioner experience.
- A short answer is useful. "Our pipeline reopens labels after the dispute window" is enough to start a thread.

## What not to share

- Only public or general information. Do not share confidential information from a current or former employer or client.
- No personal data about anyone, and no non-public details of a specific incident, customer or account.
- If you are unsure whether something is public, describe it in general terms or leave it out.

## How comments are handled

- Every comment gets a reply. Comments may be grouped, and duplicates are linked.
- Each comment is logged with how it was resolved: changed, explained or not adopted, with the reason.
- Accepted changes go into version 1.1. The feedback window is planned to run until about 30 November 2026.
- Factual errors may be corrected sooner in a correction version.

## Tracking changes for the next version

The [version 1.1 revision tracker](https://github.com/matkowsky/payment-fraud/issues/2) records author-originated proposals as well as contributor feedback. Open a focused issue when an item needs its own discussion and link it from the tracker. Include the affected version and passage, evidence, proposed revision and verification needed.

Keep proposed, accepted, implemented and verified changes distinct, and record why an item was deferred or not adopted. AI-assisted review findings require evidence checks before adoption. Actual manuscript and supplement edits should use pull requests linked to the relevant issue so contributors can review the diff and checks. The published files under `paper/v1.0/` stay intact; the line-by-line review PR #1 is never merged.

## How contributions are credited in version 1.1

- **Acknowledgment:** comments, corrections, answers to the questions, or pointers to prior work that change the text.
- **Co-authorship:** a substantial contribution to the specification itself, such as drafting or redesigning a section, designing or running an evaluation, or analysis that changes a requirement; plus reviewing the final text and agreeing to be accountable for it.
- Nothing is credited by name without your permission. You can choose to be acknowledged anonymously, or not at all.
