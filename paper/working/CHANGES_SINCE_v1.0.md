**Changed, under "5.3 Preserve meaning across stores, time and relationships":**

\*\*Channel state differs from report presence and access.\*\* For each affected item, channel and read time, the records distinguish an open channel, a temporary delay, a permanent closure and unknown state. A missing report matures only after a defined window that remained open and was fully covered. If a decision changed the channel's meaning, as a liability shift after successful authentication does \(Section 7.1\), a matured absence is read under that changed meaning and does not by itself support a negative for the original outcome. A merchant's network program status can also change that meaning: under one program, a merchant loses the liability shift on domestic authenticated payments until it exits the program \[@Stripe2026MonitoringPrograms\]. The records keep that status as it stood at decision time, because the guide does not say whether the loss reaches payments authenticated before the merchant entered the program. A later program entry is recorded as a rule event, and the liability status of payments authenticated before it remains unknown. A channel without a defined window never supports a negative. A matured absence is provisional, not final. Network and bank rules can reopen or extend a window after it has elapsed, as when a monitoring program lets issuers submit fraud disputes past the standard deadline \[@Stripe2026MonitoringPrograms\] or banks accept a late return \[@Stripe2026ACHTerms\]. Each such rule event is recorded as a channel-state transition with its source, rule version and learned time, although no platform decision caused it. A <ins>decision's effect on each channel is fixed when the decision is recorded; later transitions are separate records that never edit it. A </ins>negative read from a matured absence cites the rule version and read time under which it matured and is revised when a later transition reopens the channel. A report's resolution is not always final either: in rare cases an issuer files a further dispute on the same payment after acquiring new information, or a lost dispute later becomes a late win \[@Stripe2026Disputes\]. Inaccessible records remain unavailable without erasing a known channel state or an earlier report. Supplement S4.1 specifies the clock-start and transition fields.
