# Code of conduct

This repository is a review space for a research preprint. Everyone is welcome to take part.

- Critique the work, not the people. Be specific and courteous.
- Support claims with sources or reasoning where you can.
- Share only public or general information. Do not post confidential information, personal data about anyone, or details that could identify a specific customer, account or incident.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, email Jonathan@matkowsky.com.
